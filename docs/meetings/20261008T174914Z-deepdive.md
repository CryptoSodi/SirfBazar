[Expo push guidance](https://docs.expo.dev/push-notifications/faq/), [SDK54 notifications](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v54.0.0/sdk/notifications/).

## Internal Health — Architect

High severity:

- Delayed requests can retry under a newly signed-in account; delayed browser refreshes can overwrite login/logout state. Server refresh rotation is not atomic.
- Pending riders receive credentials but are rejected from the protected status/profile flow needed for onboarding.
- Editing a saved address can change an existing order's delivery destination.
- Cancellation can leave a rider busy or restore stock after goods have left the shop. Admin status overrides can create inconsistent assignments.

Medium severity:

- Concurrent guest-session/cart creation or quantity updates can split baskets or lose additions.
- POS/rider money formatters round fractional rupees, making displayed charges/change inaccurate.

The original eight modifications remain required. Existing recovery, signed-quote and tenant protections must be preserved. Upload-byte validation and web-push destination handling merit targeted investigation; no production exploit was attempted.
